  <section id="research" class="section-block">
    <p class="section-kicker">Research</p>
    <h2>Understanding how data-sharing ecosystems become economically viable.</h2>
    <p>My dissertation, supervised by Eric Brousseau, examines the business models of data-sharing intermediaries and the economics of data-sharing ecosystems. I work at the intersection of information systems, governance, regulation, and digital economy research.</p>

    <div class="focus-grid">
      <article>
        <h3>Data Governance and Intermediation</h3>
        <p>Data-sharing intermediaries, data spaces, ecosystem orchestration, governance design, and value creation.</p>
      </article>
      <article>
        <h3>Digital Regulation</h3>
        <p>Personal data regulation, institutional design, and comparative regulatory models across Europe, the United States, and China.</p>
      </article>
      <article>
        <h3>Platforms and Digital Economy</h3>
        <p>Markets for data, vertical AI, and sectoral or sovereign data infrastructures.</p>
      </article>
    </div>
  </section>

  <section class="section-block">
    <p class="section-kicker">Selected Publication</p>
    <h2>The Economics of Data-Sharing</h2>
    <p><strong>The Economics of Data-Sharing: An Empirical Investigation of Data Sharing Ecosystems</strong>, with Eric Brousseau and Joelle Toledano, appears in the <em>ICIS 2025 Proceedings</em>. The paper identifies economic and governance conditions that support data-sharing ecosystems over time, including investment, use-case innovation, integration complexity, risk, and incentive alignment.</p>
    <p><a href="https://aisel.aisnet.org/icis2025/sharing_econ/sharing_econ/6">View the ICIS record</a></p>
  </section>

  <section id="working-papers" class="section-block">
    <p class="section-kicker">Working Papers</p>
    <h2>Current research projects</h2>
    <div class="paper-list">
      <article>
        <h3>Interpretable Digital Traces: Mobile Wikipedia Usage and City-Level Tourism</h3>
        <p class="byline">with Paul Favier</p>
        <p>Links daily hotel room-nights to mobile and desktop Wikipedia pageviews for 704 French communes from 2018 to 2025 to build interpretable, high-frequency indicators of local tourism activity.</p>
      </article>
      <article>
        <h3>Intrinsic Data Liquidity and the Emergence of Market Governance for Data Sharing</h3>
        <p class="byline">Solo</p>
        <p>Introduces intrinsic data liquidity to explain why some data products can be exchanged through scalable markets while others require bespoke governance.</p>
      </article>
      <article>
        <h3>Models of Personal Data Regulation</h3>
        <p class="byline">with Rami Benabdelkrim and Surjasama Lahiri</p>
        <p>Studies how countries converge toward European, Chinese, or American models of personal data regulation using a comparative dataset of legal provisions across jurisdictions.</p>
      </article>
      <article>
        <h3>Emergence of Data Sharing Ecosystems in Disaster Management</h3>
        <p class="byline">with Anouk Adrot and Salome Ritouret</p>
        <p>Investigates how formal norms and bottom-up practices interact in disaster risk reduction ecosystems through interviews, NLP-assisted mapping, observation, and archival material.</p>
      </article>
    </div>
  </section>
